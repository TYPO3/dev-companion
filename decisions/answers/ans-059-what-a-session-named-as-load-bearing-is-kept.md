# D-ANS-059 — What a session named as load-bearing is kept

**The invocation notes beside the suites and the verdict checklist a triage
makes a session read stay. A session named both as what changed what it did.**

Seven feedback came out of one triage-and-patch session, and they draw a
boundary. The strengths name six tools and both skills, the failures name two,
and both of those reach a host this repository does not own. Every cost the
session named was on the side this server reads rather than computes.

## Evidence

- `feedback/2026-08-05-033954` is the strengths half, and it is specific.
  `typo3_project_describe` gave the PHP floor before the session opened a file,
  which is why the verification went through `runTests.sh` at all.
  `typo3_test_run_guide` gave the `CI=true` prefix, the `-b docker` switch and
  "a green that ran over no files is not a green". The session acted on it.
  `typo3_hint_lookup` said the expectations hide in fixtures.
  `typo3_rule_lookup` saved an unnecessary `.rst` file. `typo3_changelog_lookup`
  established that nobody had reworked the area since 2019.
- The same debrief credits both skills with a change to what the session did
  rather than a description of it. That is two verdicts instead of one wrong
  one, and a test proved red before the fix.
- The four failures are `typo3_forge_lookup` three times and
  `typo3_gerrit_lookup` once. Those are
  [`D-ANS-055`](ans-055-a-change-answers-for-an-issue-its-commit-message-names.md),
  [`D-ANS-056`](ans-056-a-search-hit-is-filled-from-the-issue-it-is.md),
  [`D-ANS-057`](ans-057-what-hangs-off-an-issue-is-named.md) and the backlog
  interface that had landed an hour before the session ran.
- The fifth cost, `feedback/2026-08-05-033924`, is a fact that lives on neither
  side. Which release lines the core maintains is not in the checkout and not in
  the corpus, and the session inferred it from 40 commits.
- No report calls anything computed here wrong. The one error the session names
  against itself is a run of host `phpunit` after it heard the floor was 8.5.
  That is a fact this server had already given it.

## Decided

- The strengths count as a boundary and not as a decision that holds. What they
  confirm is where the two kinds of answer sit. A recorded run confirms a
  decision, and none of these calls is one
  ([`D-FBK-018`](../feedback/fbk-018-a-strength-is-evidence-about-a-boundary-not-about-a-decision.md)).
- What the session named as load-bearing may not quietly drop out. That is the
  invocation notes beside the suites in `typo3_test_run_guide`, and the verdict
  checklist that `typo3-core-issue-triage` makes a session read before it
  writes.
- A reader over a foreign API owes more than a reader over the corpus. A test
  holds what it hands back against what the source said. Nothing downstream can
  tell a confident wrong answer from a right one, and both skills stop work on a
  hit.
- The three fixes are that rule applied once. This entry does not decide whether
  it generalises to the other network readers, the manuals and the contribution
  services. No evidence here says either way.

## Assumed

- One session is one read. The split is clean in these seven, and that is what
  makes it worth a record. It is not proof that the next debrief will land the
  same way.
- The costs are properties of the sources rather than of their age. Both failed
  tools are among the newest here, and a simpler account is that they have had
  the least use.

## Wrong if

- A later debrief reports a computed answer, a hint, a catalog entry, a guide,
  as the one that misled it. That would say the boundary is elsewhere.
- No report names the network readers at all after these repairs. That would say
  the split was about three defects rather than about a kind of answer.
- Somebody drops one of the named strengths and the next session reports the
  cost the note carried.

## Since then

The next debrief landed the same way, which the first **Assumed** said this
could not promise. A later triage session in the same checkout names only
computed answers as load-bearing. Those are a refusal with its cause and the
command that repairs it, and the flag that explained a failure the host
interpreter printed. And the same suite line the first debrief named, and a
changelog entry reached from a bare identifier.

One of them cuts across the split rather than along it.
