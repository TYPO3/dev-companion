# D-FBK-026 — The ladder needs an outcome that builds something

**A judgement that a capability is absent decides that it gets built, and *taken
on* is the answer it gets.**

The six answers a feedback could receive were these. Fix a sentence, move a
rule, add a routing line, or note that it already has an answer. Trim it, queue
it, or propose to leave it. None of them says *this gets built*, and the channel
exists to decide exactly that.

## Evidence

- The page opened with the statement that a feedback "is *not* a work item" and
  closed with six answers, four autonomous. The two that could carry a new
  capability hedge it. Step 1b ended "Both are queued rather than closed on the
  spot: a tool and a skill are contracts". *Queued* described the change as "too
  large for the spot", which is a size rather than a kind.
- The ladder's preamble named step 1 as the guess a lazy judgement makes. "the
  guess is always step one, which is why gaps get a fourth entry written next to
  three that already exist". Read as written, the most expensive rung is the
  suspect one and every cheaper answer is the diligent one.
- What that produced is measurable and is in `D-FBK-025`. 44 of 48 cards at
  `low`, 30 of them without a judgement. And 35 open feedback from one checkout
  that describe two skills nobody had decided to build.
- The corpus is full of feature-shaped reports that no rung welcomes. An
  API-stability lookup (`feedback/2026-08-01-115713`), a changelog index that
  reaches method names rather than titles (`115112`). A change-scope tool that
  hands `typo3_test_run_guide` the paths this server insists the caller
  determines (`122326`), Forge and Gerrit reads (`2026-08-02-144511`, `145217`,
  `145230`). A session that had just paid for its absence filed each.

## Decided

- A seventh answer, **taken on**, and it is autonomous. `decisions/` records
  that the thing gets built, what its boundary is and what would falsify the
  boundary. The card carries the first step at a priority the judgement sets,
  and the feedback stays open until the commit that ships it archives it. What
  waits is what nobody here can establish, what the tool answers about TYPO3,
  what the skill says. That is research rather than a question upward.
- *Queued* is repair of something that exists; *taken on* is a capability that
  does not. The old wording made them one rung apart in size.
- What justifies building is the corpus rather than the ask. Two sessions that
  arrive at the same shape from different tasks are the evidence. One session's
  suggestion is the weakest thing in the file. Its author guessed about this
  repository exactly as much as a judgement run guesses about TYPO3.
- *Proposed* now has to say what building would have cost and what the corpus
  behind it weighs. It is the outcome that looks like diligence from every
  angle, and it was the cheapest one to reach.

## Assumed

- That the bias was in the page rather than in the sessions that read it. The
  judgements it produced are the evidence, and they are all one way.
- That a decision before the research is safe for a shape and not for a fact. A
  tool's existence has its ground here; what it answers does not.

## Wrong if

- Something gets built that the research afterwards shows was the wrong shape: a
  tool whose verb is wrong, a skill that covers half a domain. Then the decision
  needed the research in front of it and this entry moved it behind.
- The corpus no longer tells anything apart, because every feedback becomes a
  feature. The signal is a *taken on* with one session behind it more than once.

## Since then

*Taken on* came four times in the 208 feedback judged since, three of which
shipped, and none has come back as the wrong shape.

The first **Wrong if** happened once and cost a card. One *taken on* fell the
same day, because the core's own changelogs say the marker is an input rather
than the decision. Nothing got built, which is the mechanism as it catches
itself one step before the cost rather than one step after.

The second fired in the letter: two of the four rest on one session each. What
each carries instead is an account of what the corpus does not cover, which is
not the ask this entry warned about. Four in 208 is not a corpus that no longer
tells anything apart.
