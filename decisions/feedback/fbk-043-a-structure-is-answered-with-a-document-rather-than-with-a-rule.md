# D-FBK-043 — A structure is answered with a document rather than with a rule

**Where a feedback found a structure unclear rather than a statement, the answer
is a document. `knowledge/documents/` where the caller needed it,
`documentation/` where a session here did.**

The three directories a judgement writes into carry a rule, a rationale and a
next step. A procedure whose shape is the gap fits none of them. As a
requirement it becomes one sentence that says the shape should be clear.

## Evidence

- The two pages disagreed. Step 1a of the ladder ends in prose established and
  written, while "Where the answer goes" named three directories. None of them
  is where prose lands.
- The corpus grew documents in a week the workflow never mentioned them.
  `knowledge/documents/` holds `core/contribution`, `core/testing`,
  `extension/testing` and `project/testing`, and six commits of 2026-08-03 and
  2026-08-04 added, moved or rewrapped one.
- What a document is came to a settlement in the same days and in another group.
  [`D-KNW-056`](../knowledge/knw-056-a-file-skeleton-is-shipped-as-a-version-bound-document-section.md)
  for the bound section,
  [`D-KNW-057`](../knowledge/knw-057-a-document-declares-what-it-is-and-when-to-reach-for-it.md)
  for what it declares,
  [`D-KNW-059`](../knowledge/knw-059-one-place-spells-how-a-document-is-addressed.md)
  for its address. The judgement page could reach none of it.
- A document is the only one of the four a caller reads. `typo3_rule_lookup`
  searches it and the server serves it as a resource; a requirement, a decision
  and a todo have readers on this side alone.

## Decided

- A fourth destination for what a judgement establishes, in two halves by who
  was lost. The corpus where the caller was, `documentation/` where a session at
  work here was.
- A `documentation/` page may arrive in the judgement run, under the test
  *closed on the spot* already sets. No contract moves and no lookup about TYPO3
  runs. It describes this repository, which that run has just read.
- A `knowledge/documents/` page is *taken on* instead. It states what holds
  about TYPO3, so the research is the todo's first step. The judgement decides
  only that the document exists and what it covers.
- The seven answers stay seven. This says where the established result lands,
  not what becomes of the feedback, and the invariant stands. The commit
  archives it or leaves a todo that serves it.

## Assumed

- That a judgement run can tell a structure from a statement. The distinction is
  whether the answer is one sentence somebody follows or a thing they copy. It
  comes off the feedback rather than off the category.
- That a procedure carried out here is safe to write from a judgement, because
  the evidence for it is in this checkout and nowhere else.

## Wrong if

- A document from a judgement run states something about TYPO3 that the research
  would have corrected. That is the guess with a judgement's authority the
  ladder exists to prevent, now with a published page behind it.
- Documents accumulate that nothing routes to. A page in the corpus that no
  `covers` entry names and no hint reaches is one nobody can find. The judgement
  that wrote it recorded nothing.
- Nothing reaches the three directories any more, because prose is the easier
  thing to write. A rule that belonged in `requirements/`, where a test could
  hold it, ends up as a paragraph nothing checks.

## Since then

None of the three **Wrong if** has fired. Nothing accumulates unrouted. A
`covers` entry names every document below `knowledge/documents/`, and a plain
query reaches both of the two that share a subject. The three directories get
far more than the fourth, 216 decisions and 36 requirements against 8 documents
since. So prose is not what arrives instead.

The nearest thing to the first is not a case of it. A document did state
something wrong about TYPO3, and the sentence arrived in a feature commit rather
than in a judgement run.
