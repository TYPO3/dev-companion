# D-SKL-089 — The base says what it established and what it will touch

**The order gains two statements: what the four calls established, before the
checkout is opened, and which files a change will touch, before the first
edit.**

Both were taken on the maintainer's answer rather than on a sighting here, which
is what this entry records.

## Evidence

- The order closed on what it did not reach and opened on nothing.
  `typo3-extension-health` carried the front block for its own audit, held by
  `SkillTest::theBaseIsEstablishedBeforeTheCheckoutIsOpened` for `R-SKL-004`,
  and `REVIEW-01` grades it — so a body that did not happen to say it was held
  to nothing.
- The crossings say where a session stops at another owner's files.
  `typo3-extension-health` has its finding list agreed before anything changes.
  Neither is the files a change makes of its own, and no skill named those.
- `feedback/` records no session harmed by either absence. The corpus was read
  on 2026-09-01 for a destructive edit and for an answer whose base nobody could
  see, and neither is in it.
- `R-SKL-005` puts the closing obligation after the order deliberately: a
  session taking an exemption reads the exempting paragraph least carefully.
  That argument is about where the closing one stands, not about whether a
  second may open the order.

## Decided

- Both go into `skills/base.md`, which every published skill carries, rather
  than into the three skills that report or the seven that end in a change.
  Copies are what `D-SKL-088` started counting.
- Asked before the change with the reading done, and the maintainer took both on
  2026-09-01 — the second with the reason that a deletion in somebody else's
  checkout is the one act nothing here can put back.
- `SkillTest::theOrderSaysWhatItEstablishedBeforeTheReading` and
  `SkillTest::theFilesAChangeWillTouchAreNamedBeforeTheFirstEdit` hold the two
  statements and where each stands, both seen to fail against the base without
  them.

## Assumed

- That the front block stays a few lines. It is held to what the four calls
  answered, and a block that grows into a summary of the reading is the second
  report this was written against.
- That the client's own permission layer is not already the whole of the second
  one. What a skill buys there is the announcement rather than the gate, and
  which clients gate a write is not readable from here.

## Wrong if

- Either statement is what a session drops first under pressure, so the order
  ends up with three obligations and two of them met.
- The front block is written from the reading rather than from the calls, which
  makes it a summary the report already carries.
- A session names its files and deletes something else anyway, which would say
  the announcement was never the gate.
