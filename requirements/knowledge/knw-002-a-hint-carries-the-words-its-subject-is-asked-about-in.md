# R-KNW-002 — A hint carries the words its subject is asked about in

**A hint carries the words a caller asks about its subject in, not only its file
extensions and its internal vocabulary.**

`appliesTo` is what the matcher scores, so a subject nobody can phrase their way
to is a subject the server does not have.

## From

`language-files` matched `.xlf` and `trans-unit` but neither "xlf" nor "label"
nor "language file" (2026-07-29).
