What Jambo County Council does

The council runs the services a resident uses in a year: permits, rates, waste collection, clinics and the ward offices that take an application over a counter. Each one has a page of its own, and each page says what it costs and how long it takes.

Why public participation matters

The county publishes its draft estimates before it decides them, and a forum in every ward takes comments on each one. The dates and the papers are published with the notice that opens the forum.

Contact the council

County Headquarters, Uhuru Road, Jambo Town. Monday to Friday, 08:00–17:00. A ward office takes the same applications as the headquarters.
