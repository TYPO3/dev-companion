Business permits

Apply, renew, or change one

  1. Home
  2. Services
  3. Business permits

Every business operating in Jambo County needs a single business permit for the current year. Apply online, at a ward office, or at the county headquarters.

Who needs a permit

A permit is required for any trade, service or manufacturing activity carried out in the county, including a business run from home. One permit covers one premises. A business with premises in two wards needs a permit for each.

What you need to apply

  • The registration certificate of the business
  • The national identity card or passport of the owner
  • The KRA PIN certificate
  • The plot number or the lease agreement of the premises

How long it takes

A complete application is decided within five working days. The permit is issued at the office where the application was made, and a copy is sent to the email address on the application.

Renewing a permit

Permits run to 31 December and are renewed from 2 January. A permit renewed after 31 March carries a late fee of ten per cent.
