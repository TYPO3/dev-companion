What the county runs

  1. Home
  2. Services

Every service below can be started online and finished at any ward office. The fee is the same in every ward.

Business permits

Every trade, service or manufacturing business in the county needs a single business permit for the current year. One permit covers one premises.

Land rates

Rates are billed per parcel and fall due on 31 March. A parcel number is enough to check what is owed.

Building approvals

Plans are submitted to the county physical planning office and decided by the approvals committee, which sits every second Tuesday.

Waste collection

Collection runs by ward on a published day. A missed collection is reported at a ward office or through the online form.
