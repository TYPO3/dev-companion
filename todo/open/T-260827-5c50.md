# Give the rest of the checklist items that summarize a rule their clause

The two the sightings named carry it since 2026-09-01: the changelog item in
`knowledge/task-intents.json` says which fix is casual is settled by
`core/contribution/changelog` rather than by the item, and `DEPRECATION_SWEEP`
in `src/Tool/TaskGuide.php` carries the condition it is skipped under. What is
left is the rest of `knowledge/task-intents.json` and the checklist constants
beside that one. Read each item and say whether it summarizes a rule a page
holds in full — that is a reading and not a filter, and an item stating what to
do with no page behind it takes nothing. Write the clause on the ones that do,
each naming the tool and the documentId that decides the rest, and put the edge
on the page where the page does not state it. `D-GUI-025` is what this is
weighed against, including the size the clause was weighed at.
