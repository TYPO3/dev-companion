# Ask what the record count does to the shape of an element

Ladder step 2 for one half and 1a for the other. The `extbase` hint answers
"plugin or DataProcessor" and sat in every `availableHints` index the session
received, uncalled; nothing anywhere asks what happens to the editing surface at
3101 records, which `bin/cli hints:probe` confirms reaches nothing. Both
decisions were made from the import's point of view. The step is a paragraph in
`typo3-content-element-development`'s "Choose the content model first", where
the count is an input: a bounded element renders from TypoScript and a
DataProcessor, a list with filtering, pagination or a detail view is a plugin,
and a table an editor maintains at four figures needs a module of its own. What
the thresholds are is read before it is written.
