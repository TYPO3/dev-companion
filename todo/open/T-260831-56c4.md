# Say in a hint answer that nothing matched the question

The subject landed on 2026-09-01 and the feedback is trimmed to this: six
confident, well-formed, adjacent hints read exactly like six that answer the
question, and the caller spent three calls finding out they did not. The matcher
already computes what a hit covers of the query against a floor —
`bin/cli hints:probe` prints both — and `typo3_hint_lookup` carries none of it,
so the step is which of the two an answer says: the coverage per hit, or one
line where the best of them is under some second floor naming the query terms no
hint states. It touches the matcher and a declared `outputSchema`, and the
number that decides it is a reading of the corpus rather than a choice.
