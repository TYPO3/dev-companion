# Route a relaunch to the modelling question before the scaffolding

Ladder step 3, routing: `typo3_task_guide` was given a task naming a migration
from a legacy site and answered scoped to installation and operations;
`typo3-content-element-development` did not activate for another two and three
quarter hours, by which time 136 pages had been imported into the core `html`
CType. Both workflows existed and neither reads "relaunch an existing site" as a
task whose modelling comes first. The step is the reading:
`knowledge/task-intents.json` against the words such a task arrives in —
relaunch, migrate, legacy, Bestandsinhalte — and what the brief has to open with
where one matches. The feedback also asks for a `legacy-content-relaunch` hint;
its statements are the second step and are verified before they are written.
