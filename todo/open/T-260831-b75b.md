# Name the judging documents where a skill finishes something

Ladder step 2, delivery: `any/testing/browser-check` and the six `extbase` hints
were in answers the session held and were never fetched, while every document
that produced a file was — `D-ANS-127` carries the reading and `D-ANS-128` why
the foot of an answer is not where it is repaired. The step is to put the
`documentId` beside the sentence that already asks for the judgement:
`typo3-content-element-development`'s "is looked at rather than specced first",
and `typo3-development-installation`'s "Prove it" section, which stops at status
codes. A skill body carries no `documentId` today, so what
`documentation/contributing/writing-a-skill.rst` says about naming the call that
reads a page whole is the rule to write them under, and `SkillTest` is where the
pair gets its test.
