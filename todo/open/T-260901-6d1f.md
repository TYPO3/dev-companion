# Sort the decisions nobody has been back to

`bin/cli unresolved:list` counts them and names only the oldest, because an
entry a test declares is read by whoever makes that test fail and the rest are
read by nobody — `D-DOC-054`. Take them oldest first, out of
`bin/cli decisions:list`, and give each the answer its **Wrong if** earns: a
`readings:` date where nothing fired, a **Since then** where something did,
`revoked` where the entry is wrong. What one costs was measured on `D-KNW-108`
on 2026-09-01 — read the entry, check whether the work it queued landed, re-run
the one probe or checkout reading its evidence rests on — and that entry is the
worked example of the shape. An entry written in the last week is too young for
anything to have fired, so oldest first is the whole of the order.
