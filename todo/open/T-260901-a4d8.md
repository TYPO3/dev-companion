# Have a review name the failure it shares instead of wording it again

The three failures all three forward reviews restate are now written once in
`scenarios/forward/readme.md`, measured on 2026-09-01: the corpus instead of
this thing, another context's conventions, and changing files under a
review-only request. What is left is the half that costs. A review naming one of
them edits its **How it fails**, that text is what its recorded run was judged
against, and the digest check resets the run to `unrun` — so each rewritten
review owes a fresh forward run. Three sentences against three runs is the
trade, and the moment it is worth taking is a review that is being re-run for
another reason. Take it there, one review at a time, rather than as a sweep.
