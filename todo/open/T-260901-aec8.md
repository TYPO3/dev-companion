# The commit body is prose, and nothing says so where a body is drafted

Ruled a core rule on 2026-09-01. `typo3_commit_message_guide` wraps a body,
counts its subject and names the trailers, and a body of dashes passes it
unremarked; `core/contribution/commit-messages` states what a body says and not
what it looks like.

The step is the reading first: how the core's own merged bodies are written,
counted over `.checkouts/main` rather than asserted, because the guide reports a
finding and a finding needs the form it is held against. Then the diagnostic
beside the ones that already exist in `src/Knowledge/CommitMessage.php`, at the
severity that reading supports — a body somebody wrote as a list is a style the
reviewer strikes rather than something Gerrit refuses.
