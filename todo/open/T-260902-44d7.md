# The Forge issue and the Gerrit change are one shape each in Schema

The Forge issue reference (`issue`, `subject`, `tracker`, `status`, `url`) is
declared inline three times in `ForgeLookup::outputSchema()` and once more in
`GerritLookup::outputSchema()`; the review change reference (`change`, `status`,
`url`) twice in Forge and once in Gerrit; and Forge's `terms` uses `word` and
`total` where `Schema::termCounts()` — which changelog and label answer with —
uses `term` and `matchCount`. AGENTS.md says a record shape more than one tool
answers with belongs in `TYPO3\DevCompanion\Result\Schema`. Write one builder
there per reference, an issue and a change, and use them at the seven sites;
make Forge's term counts `Schema::termCounts()`, adding the two fields rather
than renaming, since a client validates against the declared schema.
