# A hint its own vocabulary names is discarded before it is scored

`Hints::find()` narrows to the domains a call was placed in and scores what is
left, so a hint whose `appliesTo` carries the caller's words verbatim is not a
candidate at all where the domain does not match. `fluid-object-access` carried
the ViewHelper exception string while a caller quoting that string from a PHP
class reached nothing, which is what `D-KNW-075` measured on 2026-09-02 and what
one keyword answers for one message.

The question is whether a curated phrase should cross the gate on its own — a
literal `appliesTo` hit is the strongest relevance signal this corpus has, and
the domain is derived from words the caller may not know to use. What it costs
is that the domains stop bounding an answer, which is what they were narrowed
for: `D-KNW-024` and `D-KNW-033` are why a category was ever gated, and both
were written against hints reaching tasks they did not belong to.

Read `src/Knowledge/Hints.php` for where the narrowing happens, and measure
before deciding: how many hints carry an `appliesTo` phrase specific enough to
admit them alone, and what the scenario prompts under `scenarios/` return with
the gate open. This is a low priority because one phrasing is handled and the
general case has one report.
