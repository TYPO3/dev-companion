# typo3_icon_lookup says nothing about the query words that reached nothing

Taken on, `D-ANS-139`. A concept query for "paw animal pet" answered with eleven
icons the caller's own extensions register, each labelled `substring "animal"`,
and the session read the shape as a ranked concept list and stopped asking. Two
of its three words reached no icon at all and the answer does not say so.

The tool already scores term by term and writes what each matched into `why`.
First step: write the terms that matched nothing as well, as
`typo3_forge_lookup` does on a miss — its `terms` is the model, and
`Schema::termCounts()` is the declared shape.

Ranking the caller's own icons below the core's is asked for in the feedback and
is not queued: `D-ANS-139` says why, and what would change it is a second
session reporting the same read.
