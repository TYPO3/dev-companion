# Whether a quoted operand in a Fluid condition is a literal

The boundary this feedback reports is written down as `D-FBK-054` and needs
nothing further. What is left is its one knowledge-shaped tail, and it is a
claim about Fluid this repository cannot settle: that
`condition="{a} === '{b}'"` never matches because the quoted operand is a
literal, where `{a} === {b}` does.

The core writes the unquoted form — `beuser`'s Online template and `backend`'s
SiteConfiguration overview both compare two variables that way, and no core
template was found writing the quoted one. That is what the core does rather
than what Fluid does, and the difference is the whole question.

`typo3_documentation_lookup` was asked on 2026-09-02 and does not carry it: the
`f:if` reference says a condition is evaluated as a PHP expression and sends the
reader to TYPO3 Explained for complex ones, which is not this question.

So the reading is the parser's, and Fluid is in no checkout this repository
keeps — `typo3fluid/fluid` sits in the vendor tree of other projects on the
machine, and reading it there makes the evidence unreproducible for the next
session. The first step is therefore to have `bin/cli checkouts:update` keep a
`typo3fluid/fluid` checkout the way it keeps `typo3/testing-framework`, and to
read `BooleanNode` there.

Where the claim holds, it is a statement on `fluid-conditions-and-arrays`, whose
existing quoting statement is about the attribute's own delimiters and not about
this.
