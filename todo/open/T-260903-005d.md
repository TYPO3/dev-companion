# An intent whose condition the call fails contributes no checklist

Judged as `D-GUI-027`: routing, and the entry carries the evidence. **The
step.** Settle how a stated `changeType` reaches an intent, because today it
reaches one by being appended to the text `TaskIntents::detect()` matches —
which is why `changeType="cleanup"` confirms the cleanup intent on a one-file
edit, and why `audit`, `triage`, `operations` and `diagnosis` reach theirs at
all. Name the route explicitly, then hold each intent to its own `condition`
before its checklist items are appended in `TaskGuide::answer()`. What a
condition is as data — a shape the call has, rather than the sentence the entry
states — is the part to establish first, and `feedback/2026-09-03-235330` is the
second case it has to answer: an intent confirmed by words in the task text,
excluded by its own condition, four items long.
