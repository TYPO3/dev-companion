# Rewrite `requirements/` and `decisions/` in STE, then move the lead check

The records are the largest corpus: 26,201 sentences on 2026-09-14, and 402 of
the 924 leads over 25 words. `D-DOC-070` says a record changes form and never
substance: keep every number, every id, every date and every dated section.

1. Run `bin/cli prose:check` and take the rows for the two directories as the
   worklist, one group at a time.
2. Rewrite the lead first, then the body. Reread the lead after the rewrite,
   because that sentence is what a reader stops after.
3. Run `bin/cli decisions:check`, `bin/cli requirements:check` and
   `composer test` after each group. Commit each group on its own.
4. When no lead runs past 25 words, set `Prose::HELD` to `Prose::MEASURE` and
   delete the constant. Record that in a **Since then** on `D-DOC-070`.

Done when `bin/cli prose:check` names no file below the two directories and the
lead check fails at 25.
