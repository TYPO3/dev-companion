# Every tool rejects an argument it does not have

Declare `additionalProperties: false` on every tool's input schema, in one place
rather than in 28 files. The place is where `Registry::definitions()` hands the
schema to `Server\Factory`, or `Tool\Tool` where the schema originates.
`D-ANS-053` found the whole surface open and closed it on three tools alone:
`typo3_server_scope`, `typo3_documentation_lookup` and `typo3_permalink_lookup`.
On 2026-09-19 `typo3_hint_lookup` with `{"topic": 42, "bogus": 1}` answered
`isError: false` as a call without arguments. So a misspelt parameter buys a
plausible wrong answer, and the specification says a server validates every tool
input. Hold it in `tests/Contract/ToolContractTest` over every tool, and keep
`StdioServerTest::aCallNamingAnArgumentTheToolDoesNotHaveIsRejectedByThatName`
as the wire case. Check the decision's second assumption first: a client that
adds arguments of its own to a call fails from that day on. Then confirm the
decision, or write what the sweep found at its foot.
