# Read confval details and the manual list from docs.typo3.org

Since render-guides 0.45.0, docs.typo3.org serves the files this todo
waited on. `robots.txt` allows them. `manuals.json` beside
`mainmenu.json` of the docs homepage keys 35 manuals by shortcode,
each with `name`, `group`, and `versions` of `version` and `base`.
Make `Manual\Manuals` read that list in place of
`knowledge/manuals.json`, keep only the `searched` flag here, and
remove `bin/cli manuals:check`. Then make `Manual\Documentation` answer
a `std:confval` hit from `<manual base>/confvals.json`, keyed by anchor,
with `type`, `default`, and `summary` rather than a page read. An
option can also carry `versions`. A 404 means that version was
rendered before the release; on 2026-10-06, 14.3 and 13.4 still
answered 404. `viewhelpers.json` in the ViewHelper reference lists 159
ViewHelpers by tag, with class, namespace, and summary; decide whether
reading it is part of this step or a todo of its own.
