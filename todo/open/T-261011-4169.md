# Read the ViewHelpers from the reference's viewhelpers.json

The ViewHelper reference publishes no `confvals.json`. It publishes
`viewhelpers.json` at `main`, 14.3 and 13.4, keyed by tag such as `be:avatar`.
Each entry carries `class`, `namespace`, `xmlNamespace`, `summary`,
`documentation` and `docTags`. Decide first whether a ViewHelper hit of
`typo3_documentation_lookup` answers from it the way a property answers from
`confvals.json`, and what of it a caller needs beside the page. Then build that
or close the todo with the reason.
