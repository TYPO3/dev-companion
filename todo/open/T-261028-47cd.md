# The review report drifts from its line form

All three forward runs of 2026-10-28 kept the four-backtick fence and nothing
after **Not reached** inside it. All three broke the line form of the skeleton.
**Checked and correct**, **Open** and **Dropped** came as paragraphs or with a
colon for the dash, head fields carried text in parentheses, and
`assessed (partly)` stood outside the status vocabulary. Each answer also
carried a summary before the fence or a list after it, which the skeleton
neither allows nor forbids. Decide whether the skeleton states text outside the
fence, and whether a contract case or a sharper skeleton holds the form.
