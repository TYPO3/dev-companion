# Nothing asks whether an extension already does this, before a feature is built

Judged *taken on* in `D-ANS-193`: no rule asks the question (step 1a), and no
tool finds an extension by subject (step 1b). The feedback stays open until the
commit that ships both archives it.

The maintainer added on 2026-10-29: the projects use Composer packages of type
`typo3-cms-extension` as extensions. Such a package can stand on Packagist and
nowhere else. So the search reads two registries, extensions.typo3.org and
Packagist. Measured on 2026-10-29:
`https://packagist.org/search.json?q=news&type=typo3-cms-extension` answers
without a credential, with 283 results against 7112 without the type, and
`georgringer/news` first. A result names its package, its description, its
downloads and where it is published.

1. Establish whether extensions.typo3.org offers a search that a client can read
   without a credential, and what one result carries. Where it offers none,
   Packagist answers alone, and the answer says that the TER went unsearched.
2. Give `typo3_ter_lookup` a query beside `extension`, from both searches, with
   a contract case for a hit and a miss.
3. Write the hint that weighs a published extension against an own build, with
   both weights `D-ANS-193` lists. Point the `feature` item of
   `TaskGuide::CHANGE_TYPE_CHECKLIST` at it and at the search.
