# The health report gives an unverified finding no Evidence form

`skills/typo3-extension-health/references/report.md` gives a finding's
**Evidence** as verified or reasoned. The checklist's finding gate also knows an
unverified finding that names the class nobody read, and sends it to **Open**.
The skeleton has no form for it. So a session that fills in the skeleton can
file it as reasoned under a severity, which is what SKILL-09 measures against.
Give the skeleton the third form and say that it goes to **Open**. The contract
reading of 2026-10-28 found it.
