# The sitepackage checklist names a core theme 13.4 does not ship

The `sitepackage` intent in `knowledge/task-intents.json` says to lay the
package out after the theme the core ships. That theme exists from v14 alone, as
the `sitepackage-layout` hint says with `since: 14`. The checklist item carries
no range, so on 13.4 it names nothing. The skill also takes the builder as its
default start, and `D-SKL-101` leaves open which of the two trees it prefers.
Bind the item to v14, and settle the order of the builder and the core theme in
the skill and in the item. The contract reading of 2026-10-28 found it.
