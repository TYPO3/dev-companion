`TaskGuide::SURFACES_OWED` asks a session to write the surface list into its
reply before the first read. It stands in the text half of the answer alone.
Claude Code hands its model the data half (`D-EVI-011`), and the second
REVIEW-01 run of 2026-10-28 got the `surfaces` array without the sentence: it
occurs nowhere in the transcript. That is the likely reason `D-SKL-103` did not
move met 1 of REVIEW-01 in three runs. Put the instruction into the data half
beside the list, as `R-ANS-002` asks of what a client must read. Then sweep
`TaskGuide` and the other tools for further text-only instructions.
