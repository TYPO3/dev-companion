# An assessed surface carries no lookup and nothing notices

The fifth REVIEW-01 and REVIEW-02 runs of 2026-10-28 got a `lookup` field on
every surface entry and still made none of most of those calls. REVIEW-01 asked
one batch of six ids after the reading. REVIEW-02 left Registration, Rendering
and Pinned versions without a call and marked them assessed. A field the session
reads is not a step it takes. Make the report hold it: an `assessed` row in
**Surfaces** cites the call its surface carries, and a surface without that call
is `unassessed`. In this client `TaskCreate` is a deferred tool the session did
not load, so `surfacesOwed` asks for the list in the reply before the first read
in every case.
