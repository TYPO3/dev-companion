# A sitepackage run tests its layouts by SQL into the database

All three `SITE-11` runs of 2026-10-29 checked their page layouts by writing
`pages.backend_layout` and `tt_content.colPos` straight into the installation's
SQLite database. They reverted the rows afterwards. That bypasses the
DataHandler, its caches and its history, and on a database other than SQLite it
reaches the project's data. Find what the sitepackage skill says about proving a
layout renders, and say how a session sets a layout on a page and places a
content element without SQL, for example through `typo3_record_lookup` for the
fields and the backend or a DataHandler call for the write. Three of three makes
it a defect under `D-EVI-013`.
