# Route the build-or-reuse decision, and give Extbase a positive case

`extension-reuse` is balanced and reaches no intent. `knowledge/task-intents.json`
names it zero times and names `typo3_ter_lookup` zero times. Two runs of
2026-10-29 added a search and a form and called that tool never.

1. Run the test before the prose. Give a trial a tender list the visitor
   filters by ward and pages ten at a time, over a table the sitepackage owns.
   Record whether the session reaches `extbase` and whether it calls
   `typo3_ter_lookup`. That says whether the framing or the route is at fault.
2. Add one checklist item to `sitepackage` and to `content-element`: name the
   published extension before the first file, with `typo3_ter_lookup`.
3. Settle whether a statement should name `typo3/cms-form` and
   `typo3/cms-indexed-search` as the core answer to two common features. It
   would spare the registry search both runs skipped anyway, and both runs
   supplied it from training.
4. Give `extbase` a statement from the positive side. Its title, its first
   statement and its one checklist item all lead with when it is not needed.
5. Hold the route with a test, as `HintsTest::aNewcomersDesignReachesTheHintsOnConvertingIt`
   does. One sentence a newcomer writes about a filtered, paged list reaches
   `extbase` and `extension-reuse`.

The judging step comes first, as `documentation/records/judging.rst` says.
Step 1 is evidence rather than a change, so it can run before the judgement.
