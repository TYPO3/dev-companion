`bin/cli prose:check` reports the sentences in `documentation/` that run past
the measure. Rewrite each one in form and leave its substance, its ids and its
numbers. A quotation stays as its source wrote it. Keep a common adjective such
as "missing". Then extend the check in `Prose` and `ProseTest` so it fails on
`documentation/` too, and say so in AGENTS.md and in
`D-DOC-070`.
