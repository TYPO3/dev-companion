# Rewrite typo3-distribution-content for a person who reads it

Bring `skills/typo3-distribution-content/` to the shape `D-SKL-112` records. The
pilot on `typo3-core-patch-checkout` is the model. Open on what the skill covers
and where it stops, then list the steps. Give each step its instruction first
and its reason after it. Turn every enumeration into a list, with a blank line
between items that have more than one sentence. Move a rare case into a
reference that the step names with its trigger. Replace each image with a plain
word, such as "owns", "carry", "owe", "stands" or "way in". Then read every
sentence once more and ask whether a reader without this repository understands
it. Update the `SkillTest` assertions to the new wording, and keep what each one
checks. Show the skill whole to the maintainer before the commit.
