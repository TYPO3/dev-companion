# Replace the invented images in names and prose with plain words

The cache was called "the hold", and its duration `HELD_FOR`. A reader had to
decode both, and "hold" already had two senses in the glossary. Sweep `src/`,
`tests/` and the records for other words used as images where a plain word
exists. Examples: a person who "holds" an issue is its assignee, a checkout a
commit is "held against" is compared with it. Name each replacement in the
glossary where the word is this repository's own. Ask the maintainer before a
rename that reaches a name outside this checkout, such as a tool argument.
