# Judge what nothing has answered for

Give every entry the listing marks as unnamed one of two answers. A todo below
that takes it on, or a `judged:` date in its front matter. The date says a
session read it and left it as it is. The reason goes where the entry already
keeps it, the sentence **Held by** owes, or the decision it rests on. The date
is what stops the next session from the same judgement again. The judgement
checks nothing and builds nothing. Three states mean unfinished and none of them
can make a check fail. A requirement marked **open**, one held by `not guarded`,
a decision still `open` whose **Wrong if** nobody has been back to. That is why
they went unread for as long as they existed, and why a requirement sat there
unbuilt from the directory's first day. A requirement no test can hold is a
legitimate answer; one nobody has judged is not.

The decisions this asks about are the ones no test declares. Whoever makes a
test fail reads the entry it holds, because the failure prints it (`D-DOC-054`).
So the listing names the oldest of the rest and this todo points at those.
