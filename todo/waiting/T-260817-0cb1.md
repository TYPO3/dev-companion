# Ask whether a build workflow closes on a gate

Nothing gets built until the question comes back, and the priority stays `low`
because the three examples behind it have their answers. `D-SKL-044` gave step 5
of `typo3-development-installation` a clause per hint id. `D-KNW-087` made a
listed neighbour say what it prevents. `D-SKL-045` put both testing guide ids on
the browser bullet of `typo3-content-element-development`. The answer, or either
trigger fired, is what starts the work. The triggers are a session that fetches
one id and stops, or one that ships a view unverified with the guide named at
the step. Its first question is a placement: whether the closing step re-reads
`references/checklist.md`, which that skill already ships, or states its own
list.

The question now has more behind it than on the day of the ask. `D-SKL-049`'s
second **Wrong if** fired on 2026-08-18. `feedback/2026-08-18-070611` and
`feedback/2026-08-18-074327` report the same pattern from another task shape and
another directory, an extension repository booted in DDEV. Every sighting this
card came from was one sitepackage build. Both name a prescription of
`skills/base.md` rather than of a build workflow, which widens where a gate
would have to sit. The recommendation to wait stands and so does its ground. No
session has met the three corrections yet, and none of the new sightings is
about them.

A fourth shape arrived on 2026-08-28, and it is not a build workflow either.
`feedback/2026-08-28-074058` reports the deprecation sweep of `skills/base.md`
skipped across a crossing from a review into the fix. Its correction lands at
its own point of use rather than here, which is what `D-SKL-049`'s entry for
that day says. The recommendation to wait for the gate stands. Its second
trigger fired on 2026-08-31, and the answer to that sighting landed elsewhere
the next day. `feedback/2026-08-31-234929` reports a session that received the
`browser-tests` hint at 22:35 with `any/testing/browser-check` in its
`documents` array and never fetched it. By then it had built for three and a
half hours without a look at the backend. That is a view shipped unverified with
the guide named at the step, which is this card's trigger in its own words. What
answers it is `D-ANS-127` of 2026-09-01. The step that finishes something names
a judgement document, which is placement rather than a gate. So the
recommendation to wait stands and its ground has moved. The intervention against
this sighting is one day old and nothing has met it. A gate written now would be
the second answer to one report. What would start the work is a session that
ships a view unverified with the judgement document named where the work ends.

A further sighting arrived on 2026-09-04, of the fourth shape above rather than
of a new one. `feedback/2026-09-03-235407` reports the deprecation sweep of
`skills/base.md` skipped in a v14 project session that credits four other
answers by name. Its own words for it are "luck rather than judgement".
`D-ANS-147` reads it beside the second session of that day, which never fetched
a named `omittedHints` id. What both acted on arrived inside an answer, and what
both dropped was a step that told them to fetch one. That is the pattern this
card is about, from two directions in one day, and the recommendation is still
to wait. The answer to the second half landed at its own point of use on
2026-09-04, and nothing has met it.
