# Ask whether a build workflow closes on a gate

Nothing is built until the question comes back, and the priority stays `low`
because the three examples behind it are answered: `D-SKL-044` gave step 5 of
`typo3-development-installation` a clause per hint id, `D-KNW-087` made a listed
neighbour say what it prevents, and `D-SKL-045` put both testing guide ids on
the browser bullet of `typo3-content-element-development`. The answer, or either
trigger firing — a session that fetches one id and stops, or one that ships a
view unverified with the guide named at the step — is what starts the work, and
its first question is a placement: whether the closing step re-reads
`references/checklist.md`, which that skill already ships, or states its own
list.

The question now has more behind it than it was asked with. `D-SKL-049`'s second
**Wrong if** fired on 2026-08-18: `feedback/2026-08-18-070611` and
`feedback/2026-08-18-074327` report the same pattern from another task shape and
another directory — an extension repository booted in DDEV — where every
sighting this card was written from came from one sitepackage build. Both name a
prescription of `skills/base.md` rather than of a build workflow, which widens
where a gate would have to sit. The recommendation to wait is unchanged and its
ground is unchanged: the three corrections are still unmet by any session, and
none of the new sightings is about them.

A fourth shape arrived on 2026-08-28, and it is not a build workflow either:
`feedback/2026-08-28-074058` reports the deprecation sweep of `skills/base.md`
skipped across a crossing from a review into the fix. It is being taken at its
own point of use rather than here, which is what `D-SKL-049`'s entry for that
day says; the recommendation to wait for the gate is unchanged. Its second
trigger fired on 2026-08-31, and the answer to that sighting was taken elsewhere
the next day. `feedback/2026-08-31-234929` reports a session that received the
`browser-tests` hint at 22:35 with `any/testing/browser-check` in its
`documents` array, never fetched it, and had by then built for three and a half
hours without opening the backend — a view shipped unverified with the guide
named at the step, which is this card's trigger in its own words. What answers
it is `D-ANS-127` of 2026-09-01: a judging document is named at the step that
finishes something, which is placement rather than a gate. So the recommendation
to wait is unchanged and its ground has moved — the intervention against this
sighting is one day old and nothing has met it. A gate written now would be the
second answer to one report, and what would start the work is a session that
ships a view unverified with the judging document named where the work ends.
