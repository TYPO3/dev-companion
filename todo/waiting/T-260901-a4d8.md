# Have a review name the failure it shares instead of wording it again

The three failures all three forward reviews restate now stand once in
`scenarios/forward/readme.md`, measured on 2026-09-01. The corpus instead of
this thing, another context's conventions, and changed files under a review-only
request. What remains is the half that costs. A review that names one of them
edits its **How it fails**. That text is what its recorded run stands judged
against, and the digest check resets the run to `unrun`. So each rewritten
review owes a fresh forward run. Three sentences against three runs is the
trade, and the moment it is worth it is a review that re-runs for another
reason. Take it there, one review at a time, rather than as a sweep.
