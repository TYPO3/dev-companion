# Replace the invented images in names and prose with plain words

The cache was called "the hold", and its duration `HELD_FOR`. The sweep of
`src/`, `tests/` and `knowledge/` on 2026-11-27 replaced the images it found. A
person "holding" an issue is now assigned it, and a thing "held against" another
is compared with it. A cache entry is cached rather than held, and a caller
"holding" a value has it. The glossary entry `hold` names those plain words.

Two parts remain. The first is the two output fields in `waitingOn`. The second
is the same sweep over `decisions/`, `requirements/` and `todo/`, where
`grep -rnEi "\bhold\w*\b[^.]{0,60}\bagainst\b"` lists the "held against" images
and `on the hook` an idiom. A record changes in form and never in substance.
`documentation/server/tools/typo3_server_scope.rst` still says "hold that commit
against your own HEAD" in a recorded answer. It changes when somebody records
that tool again, not by hand.

"Holds" in the sense "contains", as in "one page holds 100 items", stays. It is
ordinary English, and a reader reads it at once.
